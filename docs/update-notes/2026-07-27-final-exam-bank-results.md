## Graph / SVG questions

## Formula verification method

Every new formula question's correct answer and all three numeric
distractors were computed programmatically in Python (via a standalone
`build_questions.py` script with inline `assert` statements checking each
expected value before the question was finalized), covering: the monetary
policy reaction function (direct and reverse), the short-run aggregate
supply equation (direct and reverse), the leverage ratio and its magnifying
effect on percentage equity losses, the opportunity cost of holding
money/excess reserves, the fiscal multiplier (direct and reverse), the
Keynesian cross (direct, with-`r`, multi-step shock-plus-fiscal-fix, and
reverse-solve-for-`r`), the unemployment rate and participation rate (direct
and reverse), and Okun's Law (direct, reverse, and common-error diagnosis).
Every scenario uses freshly invented numbers, independently constructed
rather than reusing the final study guide's or `problemset4`'s own figures
(neither source's worked examples are copied), per the no-verbatim/
no-mad-libs rules.
